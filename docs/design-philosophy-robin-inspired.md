# Pocket Lawyer Design Philosophy

## Direction

Pocket Lawyer should feel like a legal intelligence room: calm, fast, precise, and operational. The interface borrows the confidence of enterprise legal AI sites while staying honest about the product stage. Working features appear as active surfaces. Roadmap features appear as clearly labeled "Coming Soon" modules.

## Form

Use large editorial headlines, rounded command surfaces, and line-icon cues that feel like a modern legal platform rather than a generic chatbot. The hero should communicate a question-to-answer workflow, not decorative legal imagery.

## Space

Prefer generous bands and focused cards over dense dashboards. The live scanner remains the core demo, with surrounding sections acting as guided proof: what works now, how the workflow feels, and what becomes production-grade later.

## Color

Keep a warm cream base for trust, deep navy for intelligence, emerald/teal for action, and restrained amber for roadmap/attention states. Avoid purple gradients and fake enterprise badges.

## Motion

Use lightweight CSS motion inspired by Framer Motion patterns: staged reveal, floating product surface, and transform/opacity-only interaction states. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
